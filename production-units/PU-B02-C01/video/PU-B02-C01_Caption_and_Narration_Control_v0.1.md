# PU-B02-C01 Caption and Narration Control

The authoritative narration is embedded in the speaker notes of the approved presentation. Every slide contains 80 to 109 words of audience-facing narration.

## Pronunciation and reading guide

- SRAI: say each letter, S R A I.
- RMSE: say root mean squared error on first use, then R M S E.
- MAE: say mean absolute error on first use, then M A E.
- ROC AUC: say R O C A U C.
- R squared: read R² as R squared.
- f theta of x: read fθ(x) as f theta of x.
- y hat: read ŷ as y hat.
- empirical risk: read R̂ as R hat.

## Caption controls

- Preserve minus signs, subscripts, superscripts and decimal points.
- Use `30.876`, `40.496`, `2.048`, `0.955` and `23.8%` exactly.
- Capitalize SRAI, RMSE, MAE, ROC AUC and AI.
- Caption the deliberate leakage example as rejected or invalid.
- Do not insert presenter cues such as pause, click, explain or tell the audience.
